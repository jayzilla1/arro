Know your rights

If you don't have insurance, you have the right to know what care will cost before you get it.

Get a Good Faith Estimate

Under the No Surprises Act, if you're uninsured or paying yourself, providers must give you a written estimate when you book at least 3 business days ahead. You can also ask for one any time you're shopping around.

Ask for it in writing before your visit.
Keep it. Compare it to your final bill.
If your bill is $400 or more over the estimate, you can dispute it.

Prices in Arro are estimates, not quotes. A written Good Faith Estimate is the way to lock in your price.
